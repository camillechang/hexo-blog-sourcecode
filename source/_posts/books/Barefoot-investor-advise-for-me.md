Some tips I should remember from the book `The Barefoot Investor`.

1. Banks: Choose one with no monthly fee that offers a higher interest rate.
2. Superannuation: Check the annual fee and choose a fund that invests in an ETF index.
3. Private insurance: Pay a higher excess ($500 or $750) to lower your monthly premium.
   - Do not automatically renew insurance. Compare providers and switch if necessary.
4. Property: Buy with a 20% deposit so that you do not need to pay LMI, which uses your money to protect the lender.
5. Stocks: Become a business owner and reinvest dividends where possible.

## 中文版

我应该记住的《赤脚投资者》一书中的一些建议。

1. 银行：选择不收月费且利率较高的银行。
2. 养老金：检查年费，并选择投资 ETF 指数的基金。
3. 私人保险：支付较高的免赔额（$500 或 $750），以降低每月保费。
   - 不要自动续保。比较不同保险机构，并在必要时更换。
4. 房产：支付 20% 的首付购房，这样就不必支付 LMI；LMI 是用你的钱来保护贷款机构的保险。
5. 股票：成为企业的所有者，并尽可能将股息再投资。
