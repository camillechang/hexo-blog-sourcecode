Excerpts from *Living Without Fatigue* by Takafumi Kudo (Japan).

- Drink a glass of milk in the morning to improve sleep quality.
- Eat protein-rich foods in the morning, such as milk, fish, soy products, and nuts.
- If you sleep well but still feel tired upon waking, take a 20-minute nap during the day so that the day's fatigue is relieved that same day.
- When you feel upset or restless, have a kimchi hot pot.
- Do not snack after 10 p.m. Eating between 10 p.m. and 2 a.m. not only prolongs gastrointestinal activity but also reduces sleep quality, increases the metabolic burden, and makes weight gain more likely.
- To avoid the temptation of late-night food, it is best not to stay up late and to go to bed early.
- Around 2 p.m., when you are less likely to gain weight, you may have a snack.
- After 3 p.m., replace plain water with lemon water or other tart fruits.
- Do not snack between meals. Feeling hungry causes the stomach to secrete ghrelin, which activates mitochondria and rejuvenates cells.
- High-GI (glycemic index) foods, such as toast, rice, and potatoes, can increase fatigue. Eat more brown staple foods such as brown rice and whole-wheat bread.
- Beer can relieve premenstrual syndrome and help prevent hair loss and graying. Drink no more than 350 mL per day.
- Finish dinner before 9 p.m. and choose low-fat foods.

## 中文版

摘抄自《不疲劳的生活》，作者：[日]工藤孝文。

- 早上喝一杯牛奶，提高睡眠质量。
- 早上吃富含蛋白质的食物，如牛奶、鱼类、豆制品和坚果等。
- 建议睡得很好但醒来依然觉得累的人，白天小憩 20 分钟，当天的疲劳当天消除。
- 心烦意乱的时候吃个泡菜锅吧。
- 晚上 10 点后不要加餐。晚 10 点至凌晨 2 点吃东西，不仅会延长肠胃蠕动时间，还会导致睡眠质量下降、增加代谢负担，并且更容易发胖。
- 为了避开深夜的美食诱惑，最好不要熬夜，早点睡觉。
- 下午 2 点前后不易发胖，可以加餐。
- 下午 3 点后，用柠檬水或其他酸味水果代替凉白开。
- 三餐之间不要加餐。空腹感会使胃分泌胃饥饿素，激活线粒体，使细胞重返年轻。
- 高 GI（升糖指数）食品会增加疲惫感，例如吐司面包、大米和土豆。多吃糙米、全麦面包等茶色主食。
- 啤酒能缓解经前综合征，预防脱发、白发等，一天最多 350 mL。
- 晚餐在晚上 9 点前吃完，选择脂肪少的食物。
