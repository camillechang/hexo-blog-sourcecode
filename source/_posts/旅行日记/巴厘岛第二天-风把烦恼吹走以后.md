来巴厘岛以前，尤其是准备住在水明漾的时候，我以为这里会到处都是帅哥美女。

真正来了以后，发现好看的人其实也没有想象中那么多。

奇怪的是，我反而慢慢平静了下来。

我没有那么想刷约会软件，也没有那么想找男人或者性爱。我好像真的进入了旅行模式：脑子不再主动想着工作，也不再一直想着谁有没有回复我的消息。

只是看看大海，吹吹风，就觉得很放松、很舒服。

偶尔，我还是会想起那个熟悉的人。虽然我知道他并没有同样地喜欢我，也许我想念的，只是那一点熟悉和曾经有过的温情。

但在海边，我忽然觉得，我的人生里有没有男人，好像也没有那么重要。

## 海浪不会停留

海边的风很大，浪也很大。

我看着海浪一次次冲过来，又一次次退回去，感觉人生也是这样。

很多艰难的事情发生过，在我们身上留下印记，然后慢慢离开。快乐也不会永远停留。也许正因为一切都会过去，那些经历才会变得值得回味。

风好像把我的烦恼都吹走了。

## 度假的意义

有时候，休假也会让我产生一点负罪感。这里的人大多在服务游客，而我们什么都不做，只是在享受他们的劳动。他们的态度却总是很好。

但我也开始明白，所谓度假，大概就是辛苦工作以后，终于允许自己停下来。正因为平时努力过，什么都不做的那几天才会显得那么舒服。

如果下半辈子一直这样度假，我可能也会觉得没有意思。该体验的都体验过以后，还能做什么呢？

我还是希望自己能对这个世界有一点贡献，希望更多人知道我，也希望自己的影响力能够变得更大。

休息不是放弃生活，而是为了回去以后继续创造。

## 人生还可以是另一种样子

我越来越觉得，生命的意义不只在于活了多长时间，也在于活着的时候有没有真正快乐过，有没有觉得这一切值得。

在家里一直待着的时候，我有时会觉得无聊，觉得人生好像就这样了。但走出来以后才发现，人生原来还可以是另外一种样子。

七天的假期对我来说也许刚刚好。足够让我停下来，看看海，也重新看看自己。剩下的时间，我还是想回去认真创造。

人生的下一个目标，大概是学摄影，好好练习骑摩托车，再认真做一个自己的副业。

这趟旅行还没有结束，但我好像已经找回了一点什么。

不是男人，也不是答案。

是我自己。

## English Version

### Bali Day Two: After the Wind Carried My Worries Away

Before coming to Bali—especially before staying in Seminyak—I imagined that beautiful people would be everywhere.

Once I arrived, I realised there were not as many as I had expected.

Strangely, that made me feel calmer.

I no longer felt such a strong urge to check dating apps or look for men or sex. I had finally entered holiday mode. My mind stopped returning to work and stopped wondering whether someone had replied to my messages.

Looking at the ocean and feeling the wind was enough.

I still occasionally thought about someone familiar. I know he does not feel the same way about me. Perhaps what I miss is simply the familiarity and those brief moments of warmth.

But beside the ocean, I suddenly felt that whether or not I had a man in my life was not so important.

#### Waves Never Stay

The wind was strong, and the waves were enormous.

I watched them arrive and retreat again and again. Life began to feel the same way.

Difficult things happen, leave marks on us, and eventually move on. Happiness never lasts forever either. Perhaps experiences become precious precisely because none of them can stay.

It felt as though the wind had carried all my worries away.

#### Why We Take Holidays

Sometimes being on holiday makes me feel slightly guilty. So many people here work to serve visitors while we do nothing except enjoy ourselves—and still, they treat us with such kindness.

But perhaps that is the point of a holiday. After working hard, we finally give ourselves permission to stop. Doing nothing feels wonderful because it is different from our everyday lives.

If I spent the rest of my life on holiday, however, I would probably lose interest. Once I had tried everything, what would remain?

I still want to contribute something to the world. I want my voice to reach more people and to create a greater positive impact.

Resting is not giving up on life. It allows us to return and create again.

#### Life Can Look Different

I am beginning to believe that the value of life is not only measured by its length. It is also measured by whether we experienced real happiness and whether being alive felt worthwhile.

When I stay at home for too long, I sometimes become bored and think, “Is this all there is?” Travelling reminds me that life can take another shape.

Seven days away may be exactly enough for me: enough time to stop, look at the ocean, and see myself more clearly. Afterwards, I want to return to creating.

My next goals might be to learn photography, become confident riding my motorcycle, and seriously build a side business of my own.

This journey is not over, but I feel that I have already found something again.

Not a man. Not an answer.

Myself.
