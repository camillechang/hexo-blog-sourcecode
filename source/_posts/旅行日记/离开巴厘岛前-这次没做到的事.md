每次旅行结束前，好像都会想起一些遗憾。

不是后悔自己来了，而是到了一个新的地方以后，才发现还有那么多不会的事情，还有那么多本来可以体验、却没有来得及做的事。

## 这次在巴厘岛没有做到的事

- 没带几件漂亮衣服，也不太敢像一些外国女生那样大胆穿自己喜欢的衣服
- 不太会拍照，很多好看的瞬间没有留下来
- 健身房去得不够多
- 没有骑摩托车去更多地方，其实坐在摩托车上真的很自由
- 还会想起那个约会对象，没有彻底把他放下
- 没有好好看书
- 没有主动学习跳舞，下次蹦迪的时候还是只会跟着音乐乱跳

写下来以后，我发现这些也不完全是遗憾。

它们更像是这次旅行送给我的一张学习清单。

## 回去以后想学习的事情

- 学习摄影，至少能为自己留下好看的旅行照片
- 学习化妆和搭配，敢穿真正喜欢的衣服
- 学会游泳，让下次海岛旅行多一些选择
- 继续练习骑摩托车，拿到 full licence
- 认真发展脱口秀，让它慢慢成为副业
- 在工作上继续努力，创造更大的影响力
- 尝试其他副业，建立属于自己的东西
- 好好读书，把注意力从不喜欢我的人身上拿回来
- 主动学习跳舞，让身体更自由

我以前很容易把“不会”理解成自己不够好。

但现在觉得，不会只代表还没有学。

这次旅行让我看见了自己的遗憾，也让我看见了接下来可以去哪里。下次再来，我也许会游泳，会拍照，骑着摩托车去更远的地方，也敢穿自己真正喜欢的衣服。

至于那个不喜欢我的人，也许到那时，我已经很久没有想起他了。

旅行的意义不只是打卡多少地方。

有时候，它只是让你回来以后，更知道自己想成为谁。

## English Version

### Before Leaving Bali: What I Will Save for Next Time

Near the end of every trip, I seem to remember a few regrets.

I do not regret coming. Travelling simply reveals how many things I have not learned yet and how many experiences I could have had but did not have time—or courage—to try.

#### What I Did Not Do in Bali

- I did not bring many beautiful outfits, and I was not confident enough to dress as boldly as some of the other women
- I did not know how to take good photos, so many beautiful moments were never captured
- I did not go to the gym often enough
- I did not travel to more places by motorcycle, even though riding felt incredibly freeing
- I still thought about someone I had dated and had not completely let him go
- I did not spend much time reading
- I did not actively learn to dance, so next time I go clubbing, I will probably still be improvising

Once I wrote everything down, I realised these were not simply regrets.

They were a learning list that the journey had given me.

#### What I Want to Learn Next

- Photography, so I can create beautiful memories of my own travels
- Makeup and styling, so I feel confident wearing what I genuinely like
- Swimming, so future island trips offer more possibilities
- Motorcycle riding, until I earn my full licence
- Stand-up comedy, with the goal of gradually turning it into a side business
- How to create more influence and impact through my work
- How to build other income streams and create something of my own
- How to return my attention to books and my own life instead of someone who does not love me
- Dancing, so my body can feel freer

I used to interpret “I don't know how” as “I am not good enough.”

Now I understand that it only means I have not learned yet.

This trip showed me my regrets, but it also showed me where I could go next. When I return, perhaps I will know how to swim and take beautiful photos. Perhaps I will ride a motorcycle further, wearing the clothes I genuinely love.

As for the man who did not want me, perhaps by then I will not have thought about him for a very long time.

The meaning of travel is not only how many places we visit.

Sometimes it helps us return home knowing more clearly who we want to become.
