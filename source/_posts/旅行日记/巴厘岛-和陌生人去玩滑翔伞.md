### An Unexpected Bali Adventure: I Was Just Looking for Company—and Ended Up Paragliding

I had not updated this blog for almost two years, partly because I was caught up in an unhealthy relationship.

Recently, I realised that writing again might be a good thing. While I am alive, I want to leave behind a few memories of my life. Feelings can be so vivid in the moment and yet disappear completely with time.

So I am starting again with this journey to Bali.

I had booked a bed in a six-person hostel room. When I arrived, there was only one other woman staying there. She moved out the next day, leaving the entire room to me.

It sounded like a free upgrade, but suddenly having so much space and time to myself also left me wondering what to do.

I went online to look for another woman to explore with. After adding someone on WeChat, I eventually discovered that I would actually be meeting two men. It was their last day in Bali, and they were going paragliding.

So I went with them.

Without that chance encounter, I probably would never have chosen to try it by myself. Life can be strange: I went looking for someone to hang out with and ended up being carried into an adventure I had never planned.

Perhaps that is what travel is about.

The plan does not have to be complete, and we do not even need to speak the same language perfectly. Sometimes people simply meet, share a few hours, and then return to their separate lives.

We may never see one another again, but the view—and the surprise of that day—will stay with me.

## 中文版

因为一段不太好的关系，我已经差不多两年没有更新博客了。

现在突然觉得，重新写一点东西也挺好的。人在活着的时候，总应该为自己留下一些记忆。很多感受当下那么鲜明，过一段时间却可能什么都不记得了。

所以，我决定从这趟巴厘岛旅行开始。

## 一个人住进六人间

出发前，我在网上订了青旅的六人间。入住时，房间里只有我和另一个女生。结果第二天，她也搬走了。

于是，我一个人住进了整个六人间。

听起来像免费升级，但一个人突然空下来，也会不知道应该去哪里、做些什么。

我开始在网上找女生搭子。本来以为联系的是一个女生，加了微信以后才知道，最后见到的是两个男生。他们那天是在巴厘岛的最后一天，正准备去玩滑翔伞。

我就这样跟着去了。

如果没有遇见他们，我大概不会主动去玩滑翔伞。人生有时候真的很奇妙：你只是想找个人一起出去走走，最后却被陌生人带去完成了一件原本不在计划里的事。

旅行好像就是这样。

计划不一定完整，语言也不一定相通，但人总能在某一个短暂的时刻走到一起。大家陪伴彼此几个小时，然后又回到各自的人生。

我们可能不会再见面，但那天的风景和意外，却会留在我的记忆里。
