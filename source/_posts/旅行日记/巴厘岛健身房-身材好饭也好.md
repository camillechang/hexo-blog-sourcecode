## English Version

### A Bali Gym: Fit Bodies, Great Food, Good Life

Today, I finally checked out a gym in Bali.

There were plenty of attractive, fit people, and many of them were filming their workouts. I did not record myself, but a local Balinese woman happened to capture us in one of her videos and later sent it to me.

Luckily, I also had a workout buddy. Training with someone made the session pass much faster, and I did not spend the whole time wondering when it would end.

Looking at everyone working hard—and thinking about all the delicious, healthy food here—I had one simple thought:

Fit bodies and great food. What a good life.

I want to keep training seriously. Exercise can feel exhausting while I am doing it, but afterwards, it genuinely makes me happy.

#### My Goals for This Year

- Earn more money
- Train consistently and work towards visible abs
- Become confident riding my motorcycle and get my full licence
- Travel to different countries, see a bigger world, and meet more handsome men

Bali has made me realise how often I place unnecessary limits around my own life.

Even when travelling around, taking a motorcycle taxi feels amazing, yet I kept insisting on walking everywhere—overheating and breathing in traffic fumes along the way.

Sometimes we have to leave home before we notice that an easier, freer way is available.

Seeing a bigger world does not always require a life-changing achievement. It might simply mean walking into an unfamiliar gym, getting onto a motorcycle without fear, or realising that I can become the kind of person I once only admired.

## 中文版

今天终于打卡了巴厘岛的健身房。

健身房里的帅哥美女还挺多，很多人一边训练一边录视频。我没有给自己拍，但旁边一个巴厘岛本地女生录到了我们，后来还把视频传给了我。

还好今天有一个健身搭子。有人一起练，时间好像过得特别快，也不会一直想着什么时候才能结束。

看着周围身材很好的人认真训练，再想想这里好吃又健康的食物，突然觉得：

身材好，饭也好，真好呀。

我也要继续努力健身。运动的时候很累，但练完以后真的会让人快乐。

## 今年想完成的事情

今年的目标渐渐变得清楚了：

- 好好赚钱
- 坚持健身，争取练出六块腹肌
- 认真练习骑摩托车，拿到 full licence
- 去不同的国家旅行，看看更大的世界，也遇见更多帅哥

来到巴厘岛以后，我才发现自己以前总把生活框得太小。

就连出门也是这样。坐摩托车明明那么爽，我却总是坚持走路，把自己热得受不了，还在路上吸了一堆尾气。

有些事情，只有走出来才会发现：原来还有更轻松、更自由的方式。

看见更大的世界，不一定要完成什么惊天动地的大事。也许只是第一次走进一间陌生的健身房，第一次放心坐上摩托车，或者第一次发现，自己也可以成为以前羡慕的那种人。
