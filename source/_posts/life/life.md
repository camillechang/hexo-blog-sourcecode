## English Version

### Life — In Memory of My Beloved Uncle, 25/2/2023

- In the first half of my life, I never thought about the meaning of life. I simply followed the expected path, waiting to grow up, go to school, work, get married, and have children, as if that were simply how life was meant to unfold.

- When I was very young, I witnessed many deaths and attended many funerals, so I became especially afraid of dying. Even watching a little of a horror show would leave me too frightened to go outside or sleep at night.

- As I grew into adulthood and began to understand the world, I felt deeply insecure because I was neither a top student nor a graduate of a prestigious university, and my job was only average. I desperately wanted to meet many people and understand more of the world.

---

- It was only after going abroad that I discovered how rich and varied different lives could be. I learned that not every school requires ideological and political education, that even if you look ordinary, many people may still call you beautiful, and that you can be respected whatever work you do.

- I began picking up my broken English and slowly integrating into society. Truly becoming part of a new environment taught me that I still had to keep working for whatever I wanted.

- I also began loving myself. I started dating handsome men I had never imagined dating before and realised that making myself happy was what mattered most in life. I did not need to live through other people's eyes, and I gradually lost the urge to keep trying to understand everyone else.

- Although I still do not have much money, I am healthy and happy. I have also begun thinking about what I can do to help others and give back to society.

---

- The older I get, the more I love myself. I have also suddenly realised that time is the cruelest thing of all: someone who was vivid and alive yesterday can be in another world today, as if they had never existed. When celebrities or political leaders die, at least many records of them remain. When ordinary people disappear, it is like a drop of water entering the ocean, never to be seen again.

- In the long river of life, it turns out that death still cannot be escaped. It separates you from the people you love, making everything that happened between you feel like a dream.

- Life is unpredictable. We cannot control life and death, illness, or wealth. Even after living for many years, I am still not very good at facing death.

- Life is short, so enjoy it while you can.

I dedicate this piece to my departed loved ones. May we all have lived lives without regret.

## 中文版

### 人生——纪念我亲爱的叔叔，2023 年 2 月 25 日

- 在人生的前半场，从没想过人生的意义，只是按部就班地生活着，等待着长大、上学、工作、结婚、生子，仿佛人生本就该如此。

- 很小的时候，见过很多人的生死，参加过很多次葬礼，所以特别害怕自己死了。但凡看了一点恐怖的电视剧，晚上就吓得不敢出门、不敢睡觉。

- 在我开始成人、认识世界的时候，因为不是学霸，也不是名校毕业，工作也一般般，整个人就特别自卑，特别想认识很多人，想更多地了解这个世界。

---

- 去了国外，才发现不同人生的精彩。原来上学不是都必须学思想政治课的；原来你长相普通，很多人也会说你漂亮；无论你从事什么工作，都会受到尊重。

- 开始捡起自己破烂的英语，开始慢慢地融入社会。真正融入新的环境，才明白原来想得到什么，还是需要靠自己不断努力。

- 也开始自己爱自己，开始跟以前从未想过的帅哥们约会，开始发现人生让自己开心才最重要，没有必要活在他人的眼中，也就失去了想去了解他人的欲望。

- 虽然现在还是没有钱，但是健康、快乐。也开始想着可以做些什么，来帮助他人、回馈社会。

---

- 越长大，越爱自己，也突然意识到，时间是最残忍的。昨天还鲜活的人，今天就已经去了另一个世界，仿佛从来没有存在过。明星政要不在了，至少还有很多记录；而普通人消逝了，就像一滴水汇入了大海，再也不见踪影。

- 原来人生的长河中，躲不掉的还是死亡。它把你爱的人和你隔开，仿佛你们之间发生的一切只是一场梦。

- 人生无常，控制不了生死，控制不了疾病，控制不了财富。活了很多年，还是不太擅长应对死亡。

- 人生苦短，及时行乐。

以此文纪念我逝去的亲人，愿我们都有过不悔的人生。
