## English Version

*The Habits of the Wealthy* — Thomas C. Greey

1. Read and learn every day instead of seeking entertainment. Teach yourself and improve through biographies of successful people and books about personal growth, self-cultivation, and history.
2. Exercise consistently for more than 30 minutes every day.
3. Enjoy meeting successful people who have clear goals, optimism, enthusiasm, and a positive mindset.
4. Pursue your own dreams and goals.
5. Keep getting up early, complete the three most important tasks, and take control of your life.
6. Build multiple sources of income.
7. Have a mentor in life.
8. Maintain a positive attitude toward life.
9. Do not follow the crowd. Stand out, create your own circle, and let others join it.
10. Be polite in your conduct.
11. Help other people succeed.
12. Spend 15–30 minutes every day thinking, meditating, or jogging.
13. Ask for feedback, and do not be afraid of criticism.

## 中文版

《富有的习惯》——Thomas C. Greey

1. 每天阅读和学习，而不是娱乐。自学、提升，阅读成功人士自传以及个人修养、成长类和历史类的书。
2. 坚持运动，每天大于 30 分钟。
3. 乐于结识成功人士：目标明确、乐观热情、心态积极。
4. 追求自己的梦想和目标。
5. 坚持早起，完成最重要的 3 件事，掌控生活。
6. 建立多重收入来源。
7. 有自己的人生导师。
8. 保持积极的人生态度。
9. 不从众，让自己突出，创造你的圈子，让别人加入。
10. 举止礼貌。
11. 帮助他人成功。
12. 每天花 15–30 分钟思考、冥想和慢跑。
13. 寻求反馈，不害怕批评。
