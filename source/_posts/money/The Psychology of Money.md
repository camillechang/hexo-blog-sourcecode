- Our views about money are shaped by psychology and are subjective.
- Your personal experience with money may represent only 0.00000001% of reality, yet it can account for 80% of how you think the world works. Two equally intelligent people can therefore hold different views about how and why recessions happen, how to invest, which issues deserve priority, and how much risk they can tolerate.
- Luck and risk are like twins. They show that every outcome in life is influenced by forces beyond individual effort.
- Modern capitalism is good at producing two things: wealth and envy. Wealth motivates people to work hard, but without contentment there is no happiness in life.
- Many things in the world are not worth risking, no matter how large the potential reward.

### Wealth, Compounding, and Survival
- Compounding plus time is the key to Warren Buffett’s success.
- Becoming wealthy requires risk-taking, optimism, and the courage to make a bold move.
- Staying wealthy requires frugality and an acknowledgement that some of your wealth came from luck. Do not assume that past success can be repeated indefinitely.
- You need short-term vigilance to survive long enough to enjoy optimistic long-term outcomes.
- Anything enormous, profitable, famous, or influential results from a tail event—one event that stands out among thousands or even millions. When we focus only on its outcome, it is easy to underestimate how rare and powerful the tail event itself was.
- The principle that “a small number of things drive most results” applies not only to companies in a portfolio, but also to an investor’s own behaviour.
- Your success as an investor is determined by how you respond to occasional moments of terror, not by how you spend the many uneventful years. An investing genius should remain rational when everyone nearby becomes irrational.

### Freedom, Restraint, and Sustainable Goals
- The greatest reward of wealth is the ability to do what you want, when and where you want, with whomever you want, for as long as you want.
- Restraint is a prerequisite for becoming wealthy.
- People change. They readily recognise how much they have changed since the past but tend to underestimate how much their personality, ideas, and goals will change in the future. Psychologists call this the “End of History Illusion.”
- The first rule of compounding is never to interrupt it unnecessarily.
- At every stage of a career, set balanced goals: maintain moderate annual savings, allow yourself a reasonable amount of free time, keep commuting within reasonable limits, and spend sufficient time with family. Avoiding extremes makes a plan easier to sustain and reduces the chance of future regret.

### The Seduction of Pessimism
- For reasons I cannot understand, people always seem eager to hear that the world is coming to an end.
- The greatest dividend wealth can provide is the ability to do what you enjoy, with people you like, whenever you want and for as long as you want.

## 中文版

- 我们对金钱的观念受心理支配，具有主观性。
- 你对金钱的个人经验可能只占现实的 0.00000001%，却构成了你对世界运行方式判断的 80%。因此，两个同样聪明的人可能会对经济衰退发生的机制和原因、如何投资、哪些问题应优先处理，以及自己能承受多大风险持有不同看法。
- 运气和风险就像孪生兄弟。它们证明，生活中的每一个结果都会受到个人努力之外的力量影响。
- 现代资本主义擅长创造两样东西：财富和嫉妒。财富是人们拼命工作的动力，但如果不懂知足，生活就没有快乐。
- 世界上有许多东西不值得冒险，无论潜在收益有多大。

### 财富、复利与生存
- 复利加时间才是巴菲特成功的关键。
- 致富需要冒险精神、乐观心态，以及放手一搏的勇气。
- 守富需要节俭，也要承认财富中有一部分源于运气。不要指望过去的成功可以无限复制。
- 你需要保持短期警觉，先让自己生存下来，才能看到长期的乐观结果。
- 任何规模巨大、利润丰厚、声名远播或影响深远的事物，都源于某个尾部事件——从成千上万甚至数百万个事件中脱颖而出的一个。当我们只关注尾部事件的结果时，很容易低估事件本身的稀缺性和强大影响。
- “少数几件事决定主要结果”这一规律，不仅适用于投资组合中的公司，也适用于投资者自身的行为。
- 作为投资者，成功取决于你如何应对偶尔出现的恐惧瞬间，而不是如何度过年复一年的平淡时期。投资天才也应该能在身边所有人都失去理性时继续理性行事。

### 自由、克制与可持续的目标
- 财富最令人惊叹的回报，是让你能够在任何时间、任何地点，与任何人一起，做任何想做的事，而且想做多久就做多久。
- 克制是富有的前提。
- 人是会变的。人们能敏锐地意识到自己相比过去发生了多少变化，却容易低估自己的性格、想法和目标未来还会发生多少变化。心理学家把这一现象称为“历史终结错觉”（The End of History Illusion）。
- 复利的第一条原则是：除非万不得已，永远不要打断这个过程。
- 无论处于职业生涯的哪个阶段，都要设定均衡的目标：每年适度储蓄，给自己适量的自由时间，把通勤控制在合理范围内，并花足够的时间陪伴家人。避免任何一方面走向极端，会让计划更容易坚持，并降低未来后悔的可能。

### 悲观主义的诱惑
- 出于一些我无法理解的原因，人们似乎总喜欢听别人说世界要完蛋了。
- 财富能带来的最大红利，是让你可以随时和喜欢的人一起做喜欢的事，而且想做多久就做多久。
