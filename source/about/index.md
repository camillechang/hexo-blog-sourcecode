# Hi, I'm Camille.

I work in technology and use this space to make sense of what I learn—about cloud engineering, DevOps, money, books, habits, and life.

This is not a collection of finished answers. It is a record of ideas tested, lessons learned, and a life still in progress.

## What you'll find here

- **Technology** — practical notes from AWS, Azure, Kubernetes, Linux, and DevOps
- **Money** — investing ideas and lessons from personal-finance books
- **Books** — useful ideas I want to remember and apply
- **Life** — habits, reflections, and experiments in living better

Some posts are in English, some are in Chinese, and many are a mix of both—just like my life in Australia.
